# Data field layouts of the devices with a subscreen (roadmap 7.1)

Generated by `tools/Get-DeviceLayouts.ps1` from the simulator descriptors of SDK 9.3.0. Do not edit by hand.

The subscreen is the small round display inside the main display of these watches. The field does not know about it: it only gets the size of its draw context and the obscured edges (`DataField.getObscurityFlags()`). The simulator lists the obscured edges per field below. The rectangle of the subscreen is given in the coordinates of the simulator device image, not of the screen, so **where the subscreen overlaps a field is not in this file**: that needs a look in the simulator (roadmap 7.1, 7.4).
